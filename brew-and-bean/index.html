Our Story

Brew & Bean began as a small counter with three tables and one espresso machine. Today, it has grown into a neighbourhood favourite, but the heart of it hasn't changed — we still roast in small batches and pull every shot by hand.

We work directly with growers who share our standards for quality, and we taste every new batch before it ever reaches your cup. Good coffee takes patience, and we're happy to take the time.

Whether you're stopping in for a quick espresso or settling in with a book for the afternoon, our doors — and our coffee — are always ready for you.
